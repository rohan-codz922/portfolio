Avhaar

Civic-tech platform for public toilet cleanliness monitoring & complaint resolution

In progress — govt. deployment

Avhaar lets citizens scan a QR code at any public toilet and report a cleanliness issue in seconds — photo, description, and instant priority scoring based on upvotes. On the other side, a three-tier admin hierarchy (Area → Zone → Parent Admin) routes and resolves reports in real time, with push notifications at every status change. I designed and built the entire system — citizen app, admin dashboards, and the native Android wrapper — as founder, not contractor.

Vaidista Organics

First-ever website for an Ayurvedic skincare brand — built from scratch, storefront to admin portal

Live in production

Vaidista Organics didn't have a website before this — I designed and built the entire platform from scratch as their sole developer: storefront, cart and checkout, blog, About/contact pages, and a full admin portal the founder runs the business from day to day. The admin side generates and downloads invoices, marks items out of stock, manages the complete order lifecycle, and sends delivery confirmation emails — no spreadsheets involved. Underneath, I built the payment confirmation flow around Cashfree's webhook so an order only confirms once payment is verified, fixed a hydration race condition that could double-submit orders, shipped a discount/coupon engine, and ran a security hardening pass across the platform.
